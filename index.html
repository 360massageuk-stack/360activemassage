How Mobile Works

Very little for you to organise

I keep mobile appointments straightforward. Once we have agreed the location and time, I bring the equipment and set everything up when I arrive.

Home & Hotel Visits

I bring my massage table, fresh linens, oils and the equipment I need. A clear area roughly 3m × 2m is ideal for a full table treatment.

Workplaces & Offices

For offices or smaller spaces, Shiatsu Chair Massage can be a brilliant option. It is fully clothed, uses no oils and takes up far less room.

Couples & Longer Bookings

If I am travelling further, a longer session, a couples appointment or several people at one location can make the visit much easier to arrange.

Travel Agreed First

I will always tell you about any travel charge before the booking is confirmed. No surprise extras when I arrive.
